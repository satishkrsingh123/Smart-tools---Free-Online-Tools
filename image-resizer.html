How to Resize an Image?

1. Click on Choose Image and select an image from your device.

2. Enter the desired width and height.

3. Keep Lock aspect ratio enabled to maintain the original image proportions.

4. Select your preferred output format.

5. Click Resize Image and download your resized image.
