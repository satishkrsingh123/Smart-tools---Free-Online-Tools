How to Resize an Image?

Upload your image and choose whether you want to resize it by pixels, percentage or target file size.

Pixels: Set exact width and height.

Percentage: Reduce or increase image dimensions by percentage.

Target KB/MB: Try to reduce the output image to your desired approximate file size.

Your image is processed directly in your browser. It is not uploaded to our server.
